AI in Cybersecurity

By 2027, AI will be a standard tool in every SOC. Analysts who know how to use AI will have a significant advantage. The goal is not to become a machine learning engineer, but to use AI as a co-pilot.

Core Use Cases for AI in SOC

1. Log Summarization
AI tools like ChatGPT or Claude can take thousands of log lines and summarize the key alerts. This saves hours of manual parsing.

2. Anomaly Detection
Machine learning models can learn what normal network traffic looks like. They can detect subtle deviations that traditional signature-based rules might miss.

3. AI-Assisted Investigation
When an alert triggers, AI can analyze the surrounding logs and suggest the root cause, accelerating the triage process.
